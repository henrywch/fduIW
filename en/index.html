Who we are

The name borrows from the Arknights SideStory Hortus de Escapismo: in a wandering monastery stranded upon the barrens, its tenants still plant flowers in the cloister, day after day. We believe creation means the same — no paradise required, only a plot of flowers to live by.

Fellow travelers: bring your "escapism". The Fiction Festival and OC exhibitions open on a yearly cycle — see the Chronicle; for daily life, follow the WeChat account 「空想花庭」.
