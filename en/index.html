Who we are

The name borrows from the Arknights SideStory Hortus de Escapismo: in a wandering monastery stranded upon the barrens, a gardener keeps planting flowers. We believe creation means the same — no paradise required, only a habitable plot of flowers.

To enter the garden: bring us your unfinished world. Festivals and joint projects open on a yearly cycle — see the Chronicle; for daily life, follow the WeChat account 「空想花庭」.
